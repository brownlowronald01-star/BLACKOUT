BLACKOUT V4 — AOPG COMMUNITY WORKSPACE
======================================

START
-----
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Your board, fruit list, codes and saved items are stored locally in that browser.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
---------------
- Responsive black / crimson interface
- Adventure kanban board with drag-and-drop
- Editable fruit tier list and search
- Manual game-code tracker
- Guides, builds and saved items
- Support / donation landing section
- Sponsor and ad placement placeholders
- Partnership message copier
- Local browser storage

MONETIZATION — IMPORTANT
------------------------
The site does NOT currently process payments or serve advertisements.
No payment provider or ad network can be activated without a real account,
approved provider link, and the required policy disclosures.

To connect a donation page:
1. Choose a legitimate donation provider with a parent/guardian's help.
2. Create and verify the account and public support page.
3. Open script.js and set BLACKOUT_CONFIG.donationUrl to the HTTPS page URL.
4. Keep the URL blank until the account and page are verified.

To connect a partnership contact:
- Set BLACKOUT_CONFIG.contactEmail to a public project email address.
- Do not publish a personal/private email address.

Advertising:
- The ad area is a visual placeholder only.
- Apply to an ad network when the project meets its eligibility rules.
- Add only the provider's official approved code after reviewing its terms,
  privacy requirements, consent requirements, and age requirements.
- Clearly label sponsored content. Do not use fake ad impressions or clicks.

PREMIUM
-------
BLACKOUT Plus is a concept panel only. No paid product is being sold.
Do not charge users until features, pricing, refund terms and support are real
and clearly explained.

SAFETY / OWNERSHIP
------------------
- Do not collect card details, passwords, Roblox credentials or private data.
- Never promise in-game items or advantages in exchange for donations.
- Ask a parent/guardian to handle payment accounts and payouts if you are under 18.
- BLACKOUT is an independent fan-made project, not affiliated with Roblox or
  the AOPG developers. Use only assets you have permission to publish.
- Fruit rankings and sample code entries are placeholders and need verification.
